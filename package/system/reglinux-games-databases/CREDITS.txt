REG-Linux games databases
=========================

Redump.org
----------
redump-psx.dat, redump-ps2.dat, redump-psp.dat, redump-saturn.dat,
redump-segacd.dat and redump-dreamcast.dat are Datfiles (serial,version)
generated by and downloaded from:

  https://redump.info/downloads/

Redump is a disc preservation database and community. Its datfiles are
free to use and redistribute; see https://redump.org for details and to
support the project. regmsgd's scraper uses these to resolve an optical
disc's printed serial number directly to its known checksum(s), without
needing to hash the whole disc image first.

GameTDB
-------
gametdb-wiitdb.xml is downloaded from:

  https://www.gametdb.com/wiitdb.zip

GameTDB is a community-maintained game database covering Wii and
GameCube titles (both are folded into this single file). See
https://www.gametdb.com for details and to support the project.

MAME
----
mame-stripped.xml.gz is distilled (name, isbios/isdevice/runnable,
description, year, manufacturer, display rotation, input player count
only - ROM lists/CRCs/DIP switches/device refs all dropped) from the
official "-listxml" release matching the MAME version REG-Linux builds:

  https://github.com/mamedev/mame/releases

MAME is developed by the MAME team; see https://www.mamedev.org for
details and to support the project. regmsgd's arcade library uses this
to resolve exact screen-rotation and title/year/manufacturer/player-count
metadata for the whole MAME/FBNeo/Naomi/Atomiswave family, including on
targets that don't ship a real mame binary of their own.

These files are refreshed at build time and are not modified by REG-Linux.
All credit for their content and upkeep belongs to the Redump.org, GameTDB
and MAME communities.
